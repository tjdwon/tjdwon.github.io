# assets

Place your public files here.

- `profile.jpg` — your profile photo (shown in the hero section).
- `cv.pdf` — your CV (linked from the "View CV" button).

Until you add these files, the page shows a placeholder image for the photo,
and the "View CV" button will 404 until `cv.pdf` exists.

Note: anything in this folder is publicly accessible at
`https://tjdwon.github.io/assets/<filename>`.
