01 About

Seongwon Kang is an undergraduate at Seoul National University, double-majoring in Aerospace Engineering and Artificial Intelligence. His work centers on combining numerical methods with machine learning, including physics-informed neural networks and Discontinuous Galerkin methods, applied to aerodynamics and PDE-based simulation.
